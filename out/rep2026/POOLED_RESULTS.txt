PROTOCOL
  Chunks of 4000 sim trials / 100 EC trials, FRESH seeds per chunk
  (see scan_manifest.txt; seeds D1CEA001..A007, D1CEB001..B005,
   D1CEC001..C007, EC31A001..A004). Resumable manifest runner
  (run_chunks.sh / run_ec.sh, .done markers). Zero censored trials
  in every chunk (all "solved n/n"). The 200-trial pilot is NOT pooled
  (S3.4 methodological warning). The archived production runs (default
  seed) ARE pooled with the fresh-seed chunks - together they sample
  6-8 independent jump-table draws per configuration.

CONCLUSIONS
  1. S3.4 control: conclusion UNCHANGED, now on 3.3x the data. The
     concentration gain survives re-keying (IT_v2 1.2606 vs predicted
     1.2525, +0.7% residual; residual improved with more data); the
     DP rate returns to exactly 1/32.0; the bit-sharing gap
     (0.111 +/- 0.012) matches the overhead-formula prediction 0.105.
  2. IT_v0's fresh-seed chunks ran ~2% above the default-seed run -
     a seed-level (jump-table) effect larger than the S4.4.2 ~0.8%
     estimate; this is why the table CIs are random-effects intervals.
     Qualitative conclusions are unaffected (v0 independent-tag still
     sits between the shared-tag and SOTA values, law residual < 1%).
  3. S5.6: the real-curve SOTA+ discrepancy SHARPENED from 2.1 to
     2.5 sigma on 500 pooled trials and is not a small-sample
     artifact. It remains mechanistically open (S7.4b) and makes the
     paper's SOTA+ claims conservative (real curve better than model).
